Hello, my name is **Xin Yang(杨鑫)**. I am currently pursuing my Master’s degree at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn) under the supervision of [Prof.Binyong Sun](http://www.iasm.zju.edu.cn/2021/0525/c24255a2380363/page.htm), a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, while also working as a research intern on Baidu’s [ERNIE](https://huggingface.co/baidu) team. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from [Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/), where I was advised by [Prof.Hongming Zhu](https://cs.tongji.edu.cn/info/1063/2756.htm). Before joining Baidu, I completed internships at [NIO](https://www.nio.cn), [SAP](https://www.sap.com), [Alibaba](https://www.alibaba.com), [ByteDance](https://www.bytedance.com), [Meituan](https://www.meituan.com), and [Kuaishou](https://www.kuaishou.com). I began my career in software engineering and later shifted my focus to algorithm research, particularly Large Language Models. At Baidu, I currently work on Coding Agentic RL and OPD. My broader research interests include **LLM Post-Training**, **Agentic RL**, **OPD**, **Multi-Agent Systems**, **Data Mixture Optimization**, and the analysis and mitigation of **Reward Hacking**.
