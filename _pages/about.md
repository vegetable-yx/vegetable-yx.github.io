Hello, my name is **Xin Yang**. I am currently pursuing a master’s degree at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn) under the supervision of [Prof.Binyong Sun](http://www.iasm.zju.edu.cn/2021/0525/c24255a2380363/page.htm), a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, while also working as a research intern on Baidu’s [ERNIE](https://huggingface.co/baidu) team. I received my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from [Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/), where I was advised by [Prof.Hongming Zhu](https://cs.tongji.edu.cn/info/1063/2756.htm). Before joining Baidu, I completed internships at NIO, SAP, Alibaba, ByteDance, Meituan, and Kuaishou. I began my career in software engineering and later shifted my focus to algorithm research, particularly large language models. At Baidu, I currently work on Coding Agentic RL and OPD. My broader research interests include LLM post-training, agentic reinforcement learning, OPD, multi-agent systems, data mixture optimization, and the analysis and mitigation of reward hacking.
